
The **Grounded Vortex Power Engine (Small Temple Prototype)** is an advanced energy-conversion architecture leveraging a 3D Hilbert curve ceramic lattice combined with a tri-metal conductive layer. Designed to optimize high-density localized energy pathways and thermal dissipation, this benchmark prototype integrates advanced micro-geometry ceramics with multi-metal surface engineering.

## Core Architecture & Materials
* **Substrate Matrix:** 3D Hilbert space-filling curve machined into a high-purity **Silicon Nitride ($Si_3N_4$)** ceramic lattice, providing exceptional thermal shock resistance and structural rigidity.
* **Tri-Metal Deposition Layering:**
  * **Gold ($Au$):** Outer passivation and high-conductivity contact points to prevent surface oxidation and minimize contact resistance.
  * **Silver ($Ag$):** Intermediate high-conductivity sub-layer to maximize electron mobility and skin-effect efficiency across high-frequency pathways.
  * **Copper ($Cu$):** Primary thermal-electrical bulk conductive pathway bonded to the ceramic substrate.
* **Vortex Chamber Geometry:** Multi-tiered directional plenum designed to induce controlled localized fluid/energy vector rotation, minimizing boundary-layer turbulence.

## Technical Specifications
* **Prototype Scale:** 20 cm benchmark footprint ("Small Temple" geometry).
* **Thermal Operating Range:** Up to 350°C continuous core temperature tolerance.
* **Substrate Dielectric Strength:** $>15\text{ kV/mm}$ at standard ambient conditions.
* **Conductive Path Thickness:** Micron-tolerance vapor-deposited / electroplated tri-layer stack.

## Engineering Objectives & Test Matrix
1. **Impedance Mapping:** Verify low-frequency and high-frequency electrical impedance across the Hilbert curve pathways.
2. **Thermal Dissipation Profiling:** Evaluate passive thermal gradient distribution under continuous electrical/energy throughput.
3. **Resonance Tracking:** Measure structural frequency response of the Silicon Nitride lattice under operating loads.
